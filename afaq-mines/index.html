How we work

From mine to global market

Every shipment passes through the same six stages, run by our own geologists, lab, site supervisors and warehouse teams.

01

Exploration

Field surveys, sampling and reserve evaluation by our geology team.

02

Sourcing

Lease acquisition and procurement through our network in mining regions.

03

Grading & Testing

Chemical and physical analysis for grade, purity and quality.

04

Processing

Crushing, screening and beneficiation into export-grade concentrate.

05

Storage

Weighed, documented and stored at regional warehouses.

06

Export

Port coordination and clearing via Karachi, Port Qasim and Gwadar.
