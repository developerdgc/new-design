      Quick guide

      How to turn off your stop tap

      1. Find it. Usually under the kitchen sink, near the front door, or where the water pipe comes into the house.
      2. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Don't force a stiff tap with tools.
      3. Open the cold taps to drain the water left in the pipes.
      4. Still running? Look for an outside stop tap near your boundary, then call us.
      Quick guide

      Water near electrics or a sagging ceiling

      1. Switch off the electrics at the fuse box if water is near sockets or lights, and it's safe to reach.
      2. Stay out from under a sagging ceiling. Move valuables out of the room.
      3. Catch the water with buckets and towels, and turn off the stop tap.
      4. Call 07848 298987. We'll talk you through it while the engineer travels.
