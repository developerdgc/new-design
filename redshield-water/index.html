DIY Plumbing Safety Tutorials

Quick guide

How to turn off your stop tap

  1. Find it. Usually under the kitchen sink, near the front door, or where the water pipe enters the house.
  2. Turn it clockwise (righty-tighty) until it stops. Don't force a stiff valve with tools.
  3. Open the cold taps to drain water left in the pipes.
  4. Still running? Check for an outside stop tap near the boundary, then call us.
Quick guide

Water near electrics or a ceiling bulging?

  1. Switch off electrics at the consumer unit if water is near sockets or light fittings, as long as it's safe to reach.
  2. Keep out from under a sagging ceiling. Move valuables out of the room.
  3. Catch the water with buckets and towels. Turn off the stop tap.
