# Limos 4 Less — Homepage Redesign

A simpler, more polished homepage for [limos4less.com](https://limos4less.com/). All text, images, and links come from the current website.

**Open `index.html` in a browser to view it.** Screenshots are in `preview/`.

## Structure

Same order as the live homepage:

1. Header (unchanged)
2. Hero: full width, background video from the live site, centered text
3. About Us: photo with "20+ years" badge, BWI / IAD / DCA links
4. Our Services: 3 image tiles (Airport, One Way or Round Trips, Hourly)
5. CTA: blue band "Book your airport car before you fly"
6. Occasions: black section, 6 cards
7. Stats: blue band (20+, 10M+, 40K+, 1000+)
8. Booked Rides: Trustpilot 4.7 + client logos
9. Why Customers Book With Us: 4 points around a photo
10. FAQ: photo with call card + questions
11. Blog: 3 posts
12. Footer (unchanged)

## Design

Black, brand blue, and white sections, with small gold accents. Headings use Montserrat like the live site. Content width is 1300px with 20px side padding, matching the live homepage.

The Airports and Fleet sections were removed. The client does not have its own fleet.

## Files

- `index.html` — page markup
- `styles.css` — styles (no framework)
- `assets/img/` — images taken from the current website
- `preview/` — desktop and mobile screenshots
