Order processing

Original paintings: Due to the location of the studio, please allow 4 business days to process your order. Expect an order confirmation email immediately, and an email within those 4 days to let you know your order is on the way. (I will always try to be quicker than this.)

Fine art prints: Orders are printed to order and typically processed within 14 days, with delivery times varying based on location. You’ll receive a tracking number once your order is on its way.

Often prints are shipped well within these timeframes, but there may be factors outside of my control that mean things take a little longer, especially during busy periods.

Pickup and local delivery

We do not currently offer pickup or local delivery. However, please send me an email if you are within 40 km of Pearl Beach, NSW, and we may be able to make a mutual arrangement.

Shipping

Shipping is free for all physical products, no hidden surprises! Australia Post typically takes 3–10 business days to ship to most Australian locations. If you are remotely located this may be longer.

We do not ship outside Australia at this time, but please enquire.
