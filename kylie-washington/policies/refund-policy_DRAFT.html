We want you to love the artwork you bring home. This policy explains what to do if something is not right with your order. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect items

If your artwork or print arrives damaged, is faulty, or is not what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible and keep the original packaging. We may ask for photographs of the artwork, the packaging and the damage so that we can assess the issue and, where relevant, make a claim with the carrier.

Once we have confirmed the problem, we will arrange a repair, replacement or refund in line with the Australian Consumer Law. Where a return is needed, we will cover the cost of return shipping.

Handmade work

Original artworks and handmade pieces are individually created. Variations in texture, colour and surface, and evidence of the artist’s hand, are part of the nature of the work and are not in themselves faults.

Refunds

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method.
