How do I book?

Book online up to 1 day before your pickup, or call 410-737-7600. You can also email customerservice@limos4less.com. For rides within 24 hours, please call.

Are there any extra fees?

Some trips have extras, like airport pickup fees at DCA and Dulles, extra stops, late-night pickups, child seats, or meet and greet with a name sign. Every extra is listed on your quote before you book.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

You can change your booking online up to 1 day before pickup. For changes within 24 hours, or to ask about cancellation, call 410-737-7600. Our cancellation policy is flexible.

Is the tip included?

Tipping is up to you and never required.

Do you have child seats? Can I bring my pet?

Yes to both. Tell us the child's age when you book, and a small seat charge is shown on your quote. Pets ride free. Please tell us in advance.

Can I request a specific driver or car?

Yes. Tell us what you'd like and we will do our best, depending on availability.
