About Limos 4 Less

Chauffeured Car Service Across Maryland, DC, and Northern Virginia

Every ride is a reserved sedan or SUV with one named chauffeur and a confirmed pickup time. We handle airport transfers, point to point rides between any two addresses, and hourly bookings that hold the car for a full day.

Your car is held for your trip and your chauffeur is assigned the day before. On airport runs we track your flight, so a delayed landing moves the pickup without a call from you.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. Limos 4 Less runs airport transfers to BWI, Reagan National, and Dulles, with flight tracking on every booking.

How do I book?

Book online up to 1 day before your pickup, or call 410-737-7600. For rides within 24 hours, please call.

Are chauffeurs licensed and background checked?

Yes. Every chauffeur is licensed and background checked before driving for us, and drives this region every day.

Can I book hourly services?

Yes. Our Hourly Chauffeur Service is flexible and works well for events, meetings, or multiple stops throughout the day.

Which areas do you serve?

Maryland, Washington DC, and Northern Virginia, including Silver Spring, Montgomery County, Baltimore, Columbia, Laurel, College Park, Frederick, Hagerstown, and Arlington.

Are the rides comfortable and fairly priced?

Yes. Cars are cleaned and inspected before each run, and every trip is quoted up front.
